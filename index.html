    How to play

    Aim: tap where it should land

    Drag on the course to where you want the ball to land. The ring is the landing spot, with its yardage. The dotted trail after it is how the ball should roll out, ending at the little circle (no wind, pure strike). Use the arrows for fine aim. A red guide means trees, water or out of bounds.

    Swing: three taps

    Tap the meter to start. Tap again on the gold line to hit your target distance: short of it lands short, past it flies long. Past 100% gives extra distance but a tighter window. The marker swings back: tap on the white line. Just off it fades (early) or draws (late). In the shaded zones it slices or hooks 20 to 40 yards. Only a really bad miss duffs or shanks.

    Chipping and pitching

    Within 70 yards, Shot switches between Pitch, Chip (low, then rolls like a putt) and Flop (floats high and stops dead). The meter zooms in so the gold line is easy to hit. Club choice changes the roll: a lob wedge stops, a 9 iron runs. Watch the roll-out trail and pick the club that rolls it to the hole.

    Putting

    The dashes flow downhill. The hint reads the putt for you: uphill or downhill, and which way it breaks. The yellow trail shows how the putt starts out, so aim until it curves toward the hole. Tap on the gold line for perfect pace (a foot past). After a miss it tells you how far short or past you were.

    Spin

    Tap or drag on the little ball where you want to strike it. Low is backspin (stops quickly; wedges can zip back), high is topspin (lower and more roll), and left or right draws or fades it. The aim guide bends to show it. Spin makes the timing window a little tighter. Double-tap the ball to clear it.

    Your bag

    Choose your driver, irons, wedges and ball on the title screen. The Bomber driver hits monster drives but is hard to keep straight. Tour Blades and the Spin Ball shape the ball and make it bite. Game Improvement irons and the Fairway Finder are forgiving. High Bounce wedges help from sand.

    Trees, wind and lie

    When trees are near your line, the side view shows the flight. Punch stays under the branches and High climbs over them. Wind (flag and arrow) moves the ball sideways and changes carry. Rough, pine straw and bunkers cost distance and shrink the timing window.

    Courses

    Tap the course on the title screen to switch. Augusta National has 18 holes: tall pines, white sand, Rae's Creek at Amen Corner (11–13) and very fast, tiered greens. Play all 18 or either nine.

    Penalties

    Water: one stroke, and you drop behind the hazard. Out of bounds (past the white stakes): one stroke, and you replay from the same spot. A hole ends at par + 5.

    Playing together

    On one iPad, up to 4 players pass and play. Online, up to 4 players each use their own iPad. The host taps Create game and reads out the code, everyone joins with it, and the host taps Start game. With Same time, everyone plays their own ball without waiting. Take turns plays in normal golf order.

    View and difficulty

    Pinch to zoom (or + and −). Timing on the title screen sets the swing window and how much of each putt's path you see: Casual, Standard or Pro. Pro also hides the roll-out on long shots.
