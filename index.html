How to play

Aim

Drag on the course to point the dotted line. The ring shows where a full swing carries with no wind. Use the arrows for fine aim.

Swing: three taps

Tap the meter to start. Tap again to set power: the numbers are yards. Past 100% you get extra distance but a tighter timing window. The marker then swings back: tap exactly on the white line. Early fades or slices right, late draws or hooks left.

Wind and lie

The flag and the arrow at the top show the wind. It pushes the ball sideways and changes how far it carries. Rough, deep rough and bunkers cost distance and shrink the timing window. Only wedges play well from sand.

Putting

On the green, the moving dashes flow downhill. Aim above the hole to let it break in. Two taps: start, then set the pace. The meter shows feet. Change the range for long putts.

Penalties

Water: one stroke, and you drop behind the hazard. Out of bounds (past the white stakes): one stroke, and you replay from the same spot.

Two players

Whoever is furthest from the hole plays next. The lowest score on the last hole tees off first. On two iPads, both need internet, but they can be on different Wi‑Fi.
